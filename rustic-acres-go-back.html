Hypotheses tested

Four explanations that did not survive the data

Each of these was worth checking because it was true somewhere else. None of them explains Rustic Acres.

The August firmware roll broke the registers ruled out

Tempting: four of the frozen meters last moved on the exact day they took 17032/17037. But the base rates flatten it — 6 of 32 updated meters are frozen ≥14 d (19%) against 3 of 17 that never took the roll (18%). The update day is simply when a device that had been quiet checked in and flushed one register report. No signal.

16185 is killing meters here the way it did at Ontario Place ruled out

48 meters ran 16185 and none went silent on it. Sixteen are still running it today and all sixteen are online. Against 4 of 73 at Ontario Place, this site simply did not reproduce it. Worth carrying forward: the danger there was the pending cohort, and there are only two pending devices here — lots 36 and 56 — so n is far too small to either confirm or refute that finding.

A platform-side telemetry outage produced the catch-up lumps ruled out

Twenty-two meters show a 20–38 day gap followed by a jump, which looks like one outage. Day-by-day coverage says otherwise: every day from 07-02 to 08-31 carries 33–103 device reports, with no gap anywhere. These are individual per-device reporting gaps. That distinction is what makes lot 21 an outlier instead of one row in a crowd.

Cell coverage explains the dark meters ruled out

The four dark meters ran RSRP −108 to −115 while they were alive — better than eleven meters that are online right now, the worst of which sits at −128. Their install notes say what actually happened, and none of it is radio. Carrier splits the same way: online meters spread across AT&T, Verizon and T‑Mobile with no dark-rate difference.

Raw fsm_fail_TIFOTA identifies the failed-FOTA meters confirmed useless again

Non-zero on 47 of 49 live meters here, exactly as at Ontario Place. The counters that actually separated the two terminal cases were tifota_hard_reset_cnt (5,948 and 1,225 against a site norm of 0–2) and stFotaHttpFail. One refinement worth keeping: lot 31 carries 6 tifota_fetch_err but is fully in sync on both ST and TI — it recovered, so the counter is historical. Terminal means still out of sync, not merely non-zero.
